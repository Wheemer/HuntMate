HUNTMATE HUNTING & FISHING

This product contains exactly two workspaces: Hunting and Fishing.
It does not contain Hiking, Camping, Water Sports, Backcountry, or General Outdoor.
Those activities belong only to the separate HuntMate Outdoor app.

Hunting-specific government datasets, regulations, boundaries, public-land layers, waypoints, and saved hunts remain product-local.
Fishing data remains product-local.
No shared application storage is used with HuntMate Outdoor.
